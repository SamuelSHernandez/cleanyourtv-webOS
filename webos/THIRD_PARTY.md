## Everything else

Everything else under `webos/` was written for this project and is released under [LICENSE](LICENSE) (MIT).

- **Pictures:** none are bundled.
  - App and add-on icons are drawn by `cytv_webos/build.py` at build time.
  - The aurora wallpaper is drawn by `themes/aurora/make_wallpaper.py`.
  - Theme and add-on screenshots are of this project's own screens on a real TV. They show the logos of apps
    installed on that TV, as upstream's screenshots do.
- **Fonts:** none. The launcher uses the TV's own fonts.
- **App artwork in a built package** comes from the owner's TV through `cytv-webos sync`. It stays in that
  package and is never committed.
- **Implemented from public behaviour, not from another project's code:**
  - the `ar`/IPK package layout;
  - RFC 6455 WebSocket framing;
  - LG's SSAP message shapes;
  - the Chrome DevTools protocol calls in `cytv_webos/tvinspect.py`.
